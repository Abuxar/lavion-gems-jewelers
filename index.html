Our Heritage

Since 1978

Rooted in an enduring ancestral legacy that began in 1950 under the pioneering foundation of our ancestors, Lavion Gems & Jewellers has spent over seven decades perfecting fine jewellery. Passed down with pride from generation to generation, our house has seamlessly expanded from traditional gold artistry into exquisite diamond & gemstone collections — turning passion, heritage, and dreams into everlasting heirlooms.
